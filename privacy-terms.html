Customer Protection

Policies, Shipping & Terms

1. Kenyan Shipping & Cold Chain Logistics

AFYALISHE WELLNESS LTD delivers throughout Kenya from our primary packaging hub in Nyeri. Because our Chia Yoghurt and Yacon Kefir contain active living cultures without chemical preservatives, perishable products for Nairobi and Central Kenya are dispatched via temperature-managed local courier to preserve probiotic viability.

  • Nairobi Metro: Next-day doorstep delivery (KES 350). Orders placed before 11:00 AM qualify for same-day dispatch.
  • Nyeri & Central Kenya: Dispatched within 12-24 hours via local express courier (KES 250).
  • Rest of Kenya: Dispatched via reliable parcel courier or direct matatu parcel hub within 24-48 hours (KES 450).

2. Returns & Freshness Guarantee

If any product arrives damaged or compromised during transport, please notify our Nyeri customer desk via WhatsApp (+254 759 561657) within 24 hours of receipt with a photo of the package. We will gladly dispatch an immediate replacement or issue a full credit. Due to food safety standards, unsealed perishable bottles cannot be returned once accepted.

3. Privacy Policy (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)

AFYALISHE WELLNESS LTD respects your personal data. We collect customer names, phone numbers, delivery addresses, and email addresses strictly for order fulfillment, delivery logistics, and customer support. We never sell, lease, or share your contact information with third-party advertisers. All payment transactions through M-Pesa and bank card gateways are encrypted with industry-standard 256-bit SSL protocols.

4. Nutritional & Food Disclaimer

Our products are functional natural foods designed to promote vitality and wellbeing. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Individuals with diagnosed medical conditions, pregnant mothers, or those with specific food allergies (such as our Granola which contains groundnuts) should consult their qualified healthcare provider.
