Terms, Shipping & Customer Privacy

1. Nationwide Cold Chain & Logistics

AFYALISHE WELLNESS LTD packages and dispatches all orders from our facility in Nyeri, Kenya. Our living cultured products (Chia Yoghurt and Yacon Kefir) contain live probiotics without artificial preservatives, dispatched with temperature care:

Nairobi Metro: Doorstep courier within 24 hours KES 350
Nyeri & Central Kenya: Dispatched within 12-24 hours KES 250
Rest of Kenya: Express parcel service within 24-48 hours KES 450

2. Returns & Freshness Guarantee

If any item arrives damaged or compromised during transport, please alert our customer desk on WhatsApp (+254 759 561657) within 24 hours with an image of the parcel. We promptly issue a replacement or full refund. Due to health safety regulations, unsealed perishable items cannot be accepted for return.

3. Privacy Policy (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)

Customer phone numbers, delivery addresses, and emails are processed strictly to fulfill deliveries and customer requests. We never sell, rent, or distribute customer details to third parties. All payments via M-Pesa STK push and bank cards are secured through encrypted payment gateways.

4. Food & Allergen Disclaimer

Afyalishe products are wholesome food formulations that promote vitality and support gut health. They do not replace prescribed clinical treatments. Note that our Granola contains groundnuts and pumpkin seeds. Customers with diagnosed severe allergies or special clinical requirements should review ingredient panels prior to use.
