Nourishing Everyday Vitality Through Living Kenyan Foods

AFYALISHE WELLNESS LTD is based in Nyeri, Kenya. We formulate natural food products that promote vitality and wellbeing, completely free from artificial additives, synthetic dyes, or refined corn syrups.

Mount Kenya Volcanic Soils

Mineral-rich volcanic soils around Mount Kenya and the Aberdares yield dense, active botanical ingredients. We partner directly with regional smallholder growers for raw chia, yacon, and roselle calyxes.

Living Biological Fermentation

Traditional slow biological culturing allows billions of live probiotics to thrive in our Yacon Kefir and Chia Yoghurt, multiplying nutrient absorption and soothing gastrointestinal sensitivities.

Clean Label Guarantee

Zero synthetic preservatives, artificial stabilizers, chemical emulsifiers, or hydrogenated oils. Wholesome ingredients that family members of every generation can enjoy safely.

Dr. Monica on Preventative Gut Nutrition

Watch Dr. Monica explain how dietary synbiotics (living probiotics paired with prebiotic yacon fibers) restore gut barrier integrity and sustain daily energy.

Cold-Pressed Extraction in Nyeri

At Afyalishe, our extraction facility in Nyeri uses gentle hydraulic pressure without chemical solvents or elevated temperatures. This protects heat-sensitive Alpha-Linolenic Acid (ALA Omega-3) and essential polyphenols in our Chia Oil and flour blends.
