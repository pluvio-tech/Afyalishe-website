Our Story & Heritage

Nourishing Families Through the Living Wisdom of Food

"Food as medicine"

Founded in Nyeri, Kenya, AFYALISHE WELLNESS LTD was born from a fundamental realization: genuine vitality starts in the soil and thrives in the human gut. We create natural, innovative food products that promote energy and wellbeing — completely free from artificial additives.

🏔️

Mount Kenya Highlands

Grown in mineral-rich volcanic soils around Mount Kenya and the Aberdares. We source yacon tubers, whole chia seeds, and roselle calyxes directly from local family farms.

🧪

Artisanal Fermentation

We employ traditional, slow biological fermentation to cultivate active beneficial probiotics in our Yacon Kefir and Chia Yoghurt, naturally multiplying bioavailability.

🌱

Zero Synthetic Additives

No artificial colourings, synthetic preservatives, hydrogenated oils, or refined corn syrups. Clean labels you can trust for your toddlers, teens, and elders.
